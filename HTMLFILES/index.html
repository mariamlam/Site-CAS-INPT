Our Mission

A Heart that Beats for Others

Since 2010, the INPT Social Affairs Club has been at the heart of student solidarity. With more than 200 members fueled by energy and kindness, we transform compassion into concrete projects for those who need it most throughout the Kingdom of Morocco.

By acting directly in social centers and orphanages, we don’t just offer help—we build hope. Our commitment goes beyond simple charity; we are driving real change and advancing a culture of solidarity.

Our Actions

Discover our 9 core initiatives serving the community.

Blood Drive

Organizing donation days at INPT to save lives and promote solidarity within our community.

Neqraw Jmi3

Providing complete school supplies to underprivileged children to ensure a better educational future.

Al Ihssan Caravan

Improving access to clean water and hygiene in remote rural areas.

Academic Support

Dedicated members provide tutoring to empower learners and improve their educational journeys.

Little Engineer

Introducing children to engineering and sciences through fun, hands-on educational activities.

Action Ramadan

Providing Ftour meals to those in need, sharing the warmth and solidarity of the holy month.

Aid Sghir

Offering new clothes to orphans for Aid Al-Fitr to bring them joy and dignity.

Madkhoul

Financing income-generating projects to support families in precarious situations.

Farhat Al Aid

Donating sheep to families in need to celebrate Aid Al-Adha with dignity.
